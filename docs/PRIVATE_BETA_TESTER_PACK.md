# BECOMING | Controlled Beta Tester Pack

Status: **INTERNAL ONLY. Not approved for distribution.** Updated 2026-10-28.

## Scope and safety
- Use Preview only, never Production. Do not post the Preview publicly.
- Use a private/incognito browser profile, fictional examples and no personal, medical, confidential or financial information.
- **Do not create a local email/password account** during this beta. Local accounts use a weak browser-only password hash, not secure authentication. Use guest mode only.
- Browser data can be read by people or extensions with access to that browser profile. Guest mode is not encrypted or account-protected.
- Browser storage and data exports remain on the test device; do not promise complete deletion of every backup or browser copy.
- This is self-reflection, not healthcare or crisis support. No individual responses should be collected without permission.

## Tester instructions (10-minute uncoached visit)
1. Open the owner-approved Preview URL on your phone (private browser window).
2. Without instructions, say what you think BECOMING helps you do. Record your answer separately, without sensitive details.
3. Choose **Begin in Silence**. Find one activity that interests you.
4. Mark the activity as tried. If you want, add a **fictional** one-line reflection.
5. Find that activity under **Journey Map**, then return to **Sanctuary**.
6. Report whether anything was confusing, unreadable, clipped, repeated, or hard to tap. Record device, browser and language.
7. Close the private window when finished. Do not share screenshots containing your personal information.

## Exit criteria
- Three or more genuine external testers, at least one actual iPhone Safari and one Android browser.
- Every critical privacy/data-loss issue discovered is fixed and retested.
- No critical first-session navigation blockers; results and any residual issues recorded with evidence.
- Owner separately authorizes distribution and later release. Test completion alone does not authorize Production.
